## 1.0.0

The first release of NFM, a native Mac app for Netflix.

**Watching**
- Netflix in its own window with Safari-grade FairPlay playback, native full screen and a transparent title bar that fades while you watch.
- Mini player that floats over other apps and Spaces, Picture in Picture, Fill Screen for wide films, and Keep on Top.
- TV mode: full screen, larger titles, arrow-key and game-controller navigation.

**Sound**
- Loudness Match (on by default) brings Netflix up to the level of other apps without distortion.
- Voice Boost for clearer dialogue, and Extra Boost past 100% (combined gain capped at +18 dB).
- Audio Diagnostics measures Netflix's sound and explains what it finds.

**Control**
- Now Playing, media keys and AirPods controls; keyboard shortcuts for everything; on-screen HUD; Dock menu.
- Auto-skip intros, recaps and credits; answer "Are you still watching?"; skip the profile picker.
- Quick search, Netflix links and deep links; reopens where you left off.
